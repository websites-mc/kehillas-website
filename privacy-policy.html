Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kehillas Meat & Poultry (legal name bsd poultry llc, “we,” “us”), 126 Maple Ave, Spring Valley, NY 10977, collects, uses and protects your information when you visit our website, place an order, or sign up for text messages.

Information we collect

We collect information you give us, such as your name, mobile phone number, email address, delivery address, order details, and messages you send us. If you sign up for text messages, we also record the date, time and method of your opt-in and which message types you selected.

How we use your information

We use your information to process and deliver orders, set up and service your customer account, answer your questions, send the text messages you requested (order updates, delivery notifications, customer care and, if you opted in, specials and promotions), and improve our service.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us run our business (for example, our messaging platform, payment processing and delivery), only as needed to provide our services, and when required by law. This sharing excludes text messaging originator opt-in data and consent, which is never shared with any third party (see below).

Text messaging (SMS)

When you opt in to text messages, we collect your mobile number and opt-in record to send you the messages you requested. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP (or CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE). Reply HELP for help. Msg & data rates may apply. Msg frequency varies. See our Terms of Service for full text program terms.

Data security

We use reasonable physical, administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete your information, or stop contacting you, by emailing office@kehillaspoultry.com or calling (845) 356-1110.
